NP - we’ve seen momentum of rain conservation, etc. criteria for credible blue bond?
BJ (SPO provider) - we should look into both impact and side effects. Blue finance touches upon a lot of natural habitat. 
JM (buyside) - scale up the market demand, make the market more efficient

Issuer-level assessment on Sustainable financing framework (what are issuers focusing on, what are issuers’ strategy, etc)
How well the issuers are addressing the risk. 
NP - data center. There are conversations about green/sustainable data centers. How to make them more sustainable?
JM (buyside) - the market’s job is to promote resource efficiency. We should definitely include those data center projects in the scope of ESG. There are opportunities to analyze how those projects are done from sustainability perspectives. Through those analyses, we can send market signals to promote those data center projects to be done better. 
BJ (SPO provider) - from SPO provider perspective, our assessment includes:
Test the machine that data centers operate on (effectiveness & efficiency) 
Have you / will you build renewable capacity to address sustainable needs.
Water & draught risks
NP - do you see an opportunity to finance/refinance data centers
SK - there's definitely potential. It will be great to have the assessment on not just  GHG, but also power consumption, etc. 
NP - Resilience (as in APAC) and adaptation (as in Europe) has a lot of overlapping, and resilience/climate adaptation is under-financed now in the market.
