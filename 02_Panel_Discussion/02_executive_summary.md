Investors on the panel described how the projects behind a sustainable bond influence what they buy. Nomura Asset Management distinguished between a utility financing renewable generation and one building a green headquarters, while Tokio Marine bought Yokohama’s entire ¥1.5 billion flood resilience bond after assessing three projects and estimating the losses they could prevent. Bloomberg could link each bond to its financed assets, the issuer’s baseline and the assumptions behind reported benefits so investors can judge how much a project changes the issuer’s business or its exposure to losses.
