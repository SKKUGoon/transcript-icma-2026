Demand for resilience bonds can start with investors, but issuers still need local evidence to show what the proceeds will protect. Daiwa Securities’ Kenji Serizawa said some investors had approached issuers about selling resilience bonds, while the lack of local hazard and exposure data could make issuance difficult. Tokio Marine bought Yokohama’s entire ¥1.5 billion flood resilience bond after identifying three flood-control projects and estimating the damage and insurance claims they could avert. Bloomberg could link each financed project to its location, exposed population and loss assumptions so investors can judge what the bond is expected to protect.
