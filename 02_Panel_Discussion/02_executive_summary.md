Project disclosures shaped investment decisions in both transition and resilience bonds. Nomura Asset Management’s Jason Mortimer asked for an asset’s expected operating life and business-as-usual emissions to judge lifetime savings and fossil-fuel lock-in. Tokio Marine bought Yokohama’s entire ¥1.5 billion flood resilience bond after identifying three flood-control projects and estimating the damage and insurance claims they could avert. Bloomberg could link bonds to financed assets and distinguish the baseline, modelling assumptions and expected benefits so investors can assess the issuer’s claims.
