# Panel 2 · Sustainable Finance in Asia – current status and future landscape of transition

A transition bond is easier to assess when investors can follow the money from the issuer’s plan to the business and activity it finances. Mizuho Securities’ Kotaro Sueyoshi described a lender whose framework covers seven sectors and checks both eligible activities and the companies receiving loans. JFE Steel explained why it discloses changes to its transition timetable as electricity supply, hydrogen costs and demand for green steel evolve. Bloomberg could link use-of-proceeds reports to borrower-level eligibility checks and dated changes to issuer plans, giving analysts evidence to revisit an assessment after issuance.
