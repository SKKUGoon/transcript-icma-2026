The Philippine SEC’s Grace Ayson identified a shortage of bankable adaptation projects as a constraint on private investment: some local governments lack the technical capacity to develop proposals, and investors struggle to measure long-term returns. A Tokyo Metropolitan Government film showed how an issuer can describe a resilience outcome, reporting fewer homes flooded during typhoons of comparable size after investments in flood protection. Bloomberg could link labelled bonds to the hazards their proceeds address and to the outcomes issuers report, helping investors distinguish a project’s intended benefit from evidence of its performance.
