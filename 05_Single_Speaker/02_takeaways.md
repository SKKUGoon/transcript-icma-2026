- Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund replaced four categories of corporate disclosure recognition, including integrated reports, with one sustainability disclosure assessment in fiscal 2025. Its managers use materiality as the main selection criterion.
- Equity managers for Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund identified 89 companies with excellent sustainability disclosure and 66 whose disclosure had improved significantly. Bloomberg could link bonds to successive issuer reports so credit analysts can see which material risks a company has started disclosing.
- Large- and mid-cap companies had comparable representation among those selected by Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund. Its president, Kazuto Uchida, took this as a sign that more Japanese companies are incorporating materiality into management decisions.
- Kazuto Uchida, president of Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund, expects Japan’s phased adoption of sustainability disclosure standards, beginning with the fiscal year ending March 2027, to improve comparisons across countries. Bloomberg could identify the reporting standard and fiscal period behind each issuer disclosure so analysts can select comparable peers.
- Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund evaluates sustainability investment against two objectives for the portfolio as a whole. It seeks to reduce sustainability-related risk and strengthen markets while earning the market-average return.
- Kazuto Uchida of Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund described sustainability risk analysis, stewardship and collaboration as part of the fund’s investment approach alongside allocations to environmental, social and governance indexes and funds.
- Following revisions to Japan’s Stewardship Code, the Government Pension Investment Fund updated its stewardship policy and established voting principles. It asks external managers to engage with companies and issuers, consider sustainability factors and exercise voting rights appropriately.
- Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund and Keidanren convene asset owners several times a year. Participants compare approaches to working with external managers, exercising voting rights and engaging with sustainability rating agencies.
- Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund uses the Principles for Responsible Investment and the International Corporate Governance Network to share its investment approach and discuss sustainability with peers overseas.
- Kazuto Uchida of Japan’s Government Pension Investment Fund hopes the International Capital Market Association’s work will encourage sustainable bond issuance and deepen market liquidity. He called for continued collaboration on disclosure practices that investors can rely on.
