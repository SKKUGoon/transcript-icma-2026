Comparable impact measures would help investors decide when to commit capital again, but co-investors can make reporting harder by asking the same issuer for different information. Shiko Higashi of Zenkyoren wants to see social returns across an agricultural value chain, while Pushkala Ratan of IFC described clients fielding four or five data requests a year for the same investment. Shun Machiyama of Nomura said Tokyu Land’s progress on Scope 1 and 2 emissions made its existing target too easy for a new sustainability-linked bond, so the issuer turned to Scope 3. Bloomberg could show the original target, baseline, latest reported result and measurement method together so investors can assess both ambition and comparability.
