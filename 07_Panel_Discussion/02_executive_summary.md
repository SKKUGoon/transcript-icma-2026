Investors financing the same transaction can undermine impact comparability by sending the issuer different questionnaires, the IFC representative said. A client may field four or five requests a year, while investors still struggle to compare two similar projects reporting different indicators. IFC finds renewable energy metrics relatively mature, but social projects and smaller borrowers often lack comparable underlying data. Bloomberg could map reported metrics to common definitions while retaining the issuer’s original measure, helping investors see which portfolio comparisons the evidence supports.
